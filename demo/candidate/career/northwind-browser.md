# Northwind Browser: Group Product Manager, Privacy and Permissions (2016 to 2021, fictional)

- Owned the privacy settings and the permissions prompt (camera, location, notifications) for a desktop and mobile browser used by hundreds of millions of people.
- Redesigned the permissions prompt so a site asks only when the person acts; prompts shown fell by about a third in the first quarter after launch.
- Ran each launch through privacy review with legal and with two data-protection regulators.
- Led a team of four product managers and worked with about forty engineers across two sites.
