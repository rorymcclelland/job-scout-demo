# Positioning: what may be said about Sam (fictional)

The CV is a sales document, and honesty is a hard requirement. Say plainly what the record supports; do not hedge what it supports, and do not claim what it does not.

## Say it plainly

- Led privacy and permissions settings for a browser with hundreds of millions of users (Northwind Browser).
- Took Halcyon Assets' platform to market as multi-tenant SaaS on a private cloud.
- Led discovery, bids and pilots using AI at Fieldwork Studio; builds prototypes with AI coding tools.

## Say it carefully

- Meridian Members: a **plan** with projected figures (membership growth, revenue). Never state them as achieved.
- Fieldwork Studio clients are never named.
- Sam took products to market; Sam did not sell.

## Never say (barred)

- RAG, vector search, or "production AI systems built personally".
- Any figure not in the career notes.
