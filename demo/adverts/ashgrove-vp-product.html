How we work

We are a team of people who care about doing the right thing for our customers. We work in small, cross-functional teams of product, design and engineering, and we plan in six-week cycles with a short break between them for clean-up and learning. Decisions are written down, shared openly and revisited when the facts change. We value clear writing, calm debate and kindness, and we expect leaders to coach as much as they direct.

Our hiring process

A thirty-minute call with the hiring manager, a written exercise you prepare at home, a panel conversation about the exercise, and a final conversation with two members of the leadership team. We aim to decide within three weeks and we give feedback at every stage.
