Head of Product

Corvid Labs · London (hybrid, three days in the office)

About us

Corvid Labs builds an AI assistant platform that lets companies give AI agents safe, permissioned access to their own tools and data.

What you will do

  • Lead product for the agent platform: permissions, audit and the admin console
  • Set the product strategy with the CEO and CTO, and own the roadmap
  • Grow and coach a team of four product managers
  • Work with security, legal and our largest customers on trust and privacy

What you will bring

  • Ten or more years in product management, with at least three leading product managers
  • Shipped privacy, permissions or trust features at scale
  • Experience with B2B SaaS platforms and enterprise customers
  • Strong understanding of AI products and their risks

Nice to have

  • Experience with developer platforms or APIs
  • Has worked with regulators or data-protection teams

Pay

£140,000 to £160,000 plus equity

How we work

We are a team of people who care about doing the right thing for our customers. We work in small, cross-functional teams of product, design and engineering, and we plan in six-week cycles with a short break between them for clean-up and learning. Decisions are written down, shared openly and revisited when the facts change. We value clear writing, calm debate and kindness, and we expect leaders to coach as much as they direct.

What we offer

  • Twenty-seven days of holiday plus bank holidays
  • A learning budget of two thousand pounds a year
  • Private health cover and an employee assistance programme
  • Flexible hours and a home-office allowance
  • Enhanced parental leave from day one

Our hiring process

A thirty-minute call with the hiring manager, a written exercise you prepare at home, a panel conversation about the exercise, and a final conversation with two members of the leadership team. We aim to decide within three weeks and we give feedback at every stage.
